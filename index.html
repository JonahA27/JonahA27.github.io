Case Studies & Projects

Lowe's Holmdel Organizational & Communication Gap Analysis

Conducted a comprehensive, semester-long operational analysis of the Lowe's retail branch in Holmdel, New Jersey, focusing on management practices, workplace communication, and staff retention.

  • Gathered qualitative and quantitative data by interviewing employees across various organizational hierarchy levels.
  • Assessed onboarding workflows, conflict resolution protocols, and overall operational functionality.
  • Identified a critical communication and recognition gap between store management and frontline associates.
  • Formulated strategic recommendations: implemented monthly associate recognition routines and bi-weekly supervisor check-ins to bridge the gap.
Operations Analysis Workplace Communication Process Improvement Field Research
