Semester-Long Enterprise Analysis

Lowe's Holmdel NJ: Workplace Function & Communication Analysis

Retail Operations & Management

Conducted a comprehensive company analysis of Lowe’s in Holmdel, New Jersey with a dedicated team to determine operational efficiency and employee status. The study focused on gathering qualitative and quantitative workforce data to assess management practices, onboarding processes, and workplace conflict resolution mechanisms.

Methodology & Research

  • Interviewed personnel across diverse organizational tiers to evaluate communication clarity and operational cohesion.
  • Assessed onboarding efficiency and protocols for handling high-pressure or uncomfortable workplace situations.
  • Analyzed the correlation between frontline associate morale and inventory/store execution effectiveness.

Core Finding

Identified a significant communication and recognition gap between store managers and associates. Associates frequently felt unrecognized and unappreciated for their contributions, impacting overall motivation and workflow alignment.

Actionable Recommendations

  • Launch structured monthly recognition routines to improve frontline staff morale.
  • Schedule bi-weekly supervisor-associate check-ins to streamline communication loops.
